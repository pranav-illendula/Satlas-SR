# Satlas-SR base-paper reproduction gap analysis

**Scope:** Read-only comparison of the paper’s reported methods with the local Satlas-SR implementation, configuration, released checkpoint, run notes, and existing evaluation. No inference, training, evaluation, download, or existing-artifact modification was performed.

## Executive summary

The work completed so far reproduces a useful and repeatable **inference pass** of the released eight-TCI-input, small ESRGAN checkpoint over the 256-tile `small_val_set`. All 256 manifest rows report successful inference. The checkpoint is the official `esrgan_8S2.pth`, its recorded SHA-256 is `167d6b9d5c931be06bde6cfbdd62edf5f1ac8aa9b357c4146c7e948d3f672e07`, and the run used the `params_ema` weights. The output is 4x, 128x128 RGB.

That is **not a reproduction of the paper’s complete experimental results or training**. The paper’s main method comparison uses an 87M-parameter medium ESRGAN alongside SRCNN, HighResNet, and SR3; its other experiments cover model/data scaling, CLIPScore and domain-knowledge modifications, downstream tasks, and human studies. The local checkpoint is the approximately 17M small 8-S2 model. No training run, full paper split evaluation, baseline comparison, CLIPScore/cPSNR/LPIPS result set, or paper human study is present locally.

The existing 256-tile PSNR/SSIM are **exploratory only**. Their pixel registration and temporal assumptions remain unverified, as recorded in `reports/satlas_sr_provenance_investigation.md` and the evaluation summary. Do not describe them as validated performance.

## Reproduced inference versus paper reproduction

The local work establishes that the released 8-S2 small ESRGAN checkpoint can successfully infer all 256 available small-validation tiles under the recorded runner/configuration. The checkpoint hash, EMA key, seed, frame-index selections, runtime, and output paths are recorded in `results/satlas_sr_full_validation/run_summary.md` and `inference_manifest.csv`. This is a checkpoint inference reproduction, with a documented tile-order difference from stock inference.

It does not reproduce the checkpoint’s training, the Section 5 medium-ESRGAN comparison, the paper’s data/model sweeps, alternate architectures, cross-dataset results, improved model, downstream studies, or human studies. The PSNR/SSIM summary is an exploratory local diagnostic, not the paper’s reported evaluation and not validated pixel-level performance.

## Smallest next steps, in priority order

1. **Obtain authoritative data provenance first.** Request the compact per-tile/per-frame source mapping and transforms described in `reports/satlas_sr_provenance_request.md`; verify that the exact S2 frames and NAIP targets share documented grids and meet an agreed temporal/cloud screen. Until this gate is passed, keep image metrics exploratory.
2. **Fix the reproduction target from the paper artifacts.** Obtain and inspect Supplement A.3/A.4/A.5 and identify whether the immediate target is the 87M Section 5 method-comparison ESRGAN, one of the three size-scaling models, or the Section 6 “best model.” The current small 8-S2 checkpoint is not a substitute for those distinct experiments.
3. **Reproduce only the selected experiment’s training recipe and split.** Establish its exact data fraction/split, frame policy, architecture, optimizer/loss schedule, iteration endpoint, checkpoint selection, and code/dependency revision from supplement, config, and run records. Train from the documented initialization and retain logs/checkpoints; do not infer actual run settings solely from the generic config.
4. **Recreate the matched evaluation protocol.** Use the paper’s test datasets and exact implementations/parameters (including crop and metric conventions), first on a provenance-approved subset, then at the stated scale. Keep per-tile exclusions and aggregate uncertainty visible. Reproduce comparisons/ablations or human studies only if claiming those paper findings.

If “faithfully reproduce the base paper” means every reported experiment, it is substantially larger than running the released checkpoint: it includes the four-dataset method comparison, scaling runs, CLIPScore/knowledge variants, downstream tasks, and human studies. The smallest defensible near-term objective is a provenance-verified reproduction of one explicitly selected paper experiment, not an assertion that the whole paper has been replicated.

## Conclusion

**The released small-checkpoint inference is reproduced; the base paper is not.** Model-family implementation, 8-TCI input handling, EMA checkpoint selection, 4x output, and successful 256-tile inference are supported by local artifacts. Training history, the paper’s medium ESRGAN comparison, complete metric suite/results, scaling and ablation experiments, downstream results, and human studies remain unreproduced. Spatial/temporal validation remains blocked by missing source provenance. Existing PSNR/SSIM must stay labeled exploratory.
